## Games

**Finished**

- _Clair Obscur: Expedition 33_. The best game of the year for me. Insanely good. I rarely play games as large as this, but Expedition 33 was easily worth the time I poured into it, which got to around ~70 hours. Great soundtrack, great storytelling, great environments, great gameplay. After each session, my wife and I were debating what's going on with the story, who's who et cetera. Rarely I get so drawn in to a piece of media.
